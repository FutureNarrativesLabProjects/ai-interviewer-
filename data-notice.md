# Participant Information & Data Notice
**Future Narratives Lab AI Interviewer**

---

## What is this study?

You are being invited to take part in a research interview conducted by the Future Narratives Lab. The interview is led by an AI and explores your experience of being part of a grassroots community. It typically takes 20–40 minutes to complete.

Participation is entirely voluntary. You can stop at any time by clicking **Quit**.

---

## What data is collected?

During the interview, the following information is recorded:

- **Your responses** — everything you type in the chat
- **The AI's questions** — the full conversation transcript
- **Timing information** — when you started and how long the interview took

No other personal data (name, email, location, device information) is collected unless you choose to share it in your responses.

---

## How does the AI work?

Your responses are sent to **Anthropic's Claude AI** to generate the interviewer's questions. This means your conversation is processed by Anthropic's servers in real time. Anthropic's data use is governed by their [Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy-policy) and [Usage Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/aup). The researchers do not control Anthropic's data practices.

---

## Where is data stored?

Your transcript and timing data are saved on a secure server hosted by **Streamlit Community Cloud**. Only the research team has access to these files. Data is not shared with third parties beyond the AI processing described above.

---

## How long is data kept?

Interview transcripts will be retained for the duration of the research project and deleted once the research is complete. If you would like your data removed before that point, please contact us (see below).

---

## Who has access?

| Who | Access |
|-----|--------|
| You | Your own conversation only, during the interview |
| Research team (Future Narratives Lab) | All anonymised transcripts for analysis |
| Anthropic | Processes messages in real time to generate responses |
| General public | None — transcripts are not published |

---

## Your rights

- You can stop the interview at any time without giving a reason
- You can request that your data be deleted after the interview by contacting the research team
- Results of the research will be anonymised — no individual will be identifiable in any published outputs
